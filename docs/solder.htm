Soldering (and Crimping) the Electronic Circuits and Cables/Connectors

As well as having to laser cut solder masks for the SMT row_boards and led_board, and soldering them on a hot plate, there were many through-hole components and wires to hand-solder and quite a few connectors to painstakingly crimp onto cables in this phase.

This was several days of hard work, epsecially as I decided to test each board after soldering by plugging it into the prototype breadboard circuits and found problems, some vexing and difficult to diagnose, that had to be corrected on a number of the boards before I could continue to soldering or crimping the next pcb/cable.

Finally, there was the job of removing the store-bought USBC hub from its case, and retro-fitting it into the TE3 project. In reality that sub-task had been going on for a number of weeks as the USBC hub is instrumental to the device, providing the main USBC interface to the iPad, as well as being the power supply for the entire box. I spent much time figuring out how this particular USBC hub was wired, before desoldering and removing its original cable, soldering a new cable with a JST connector onto it, adding wires and a JST connector for powering the rPi, and testing it over and over again before even committing myself to do this project.

USB Stuff

There was a decent amount of work to solder up the various USB connectors and cables. and crimp JST connectors on them, starting with removing the resistors and adding JST connectors to the two USBC female breakout boards.

Then there were two of the the home made USBA connectors going to store-bought micro connectors and their tiny soldering points. Cut all the wires, strip em', tin the wires and connectors. Then solder amd weave the wires to the connectors, and hot glue the covers together.

Then the USBA breakout boards for the two external USB ports, one to a home made USABA connector, and the other to a crimped JST4 connectors. The wires are hand-weaved, then later I started using little pieces of heatshrink to keep them neat.
