3D Printing the TE3 parts

3D Printing the parts took several days and was actually done while I was creating and soldering the PCBs. I decided to print the box out of PETG as I've had longevity problems with PLA. You can find the Fusion 360 design for the box, as well as all of the STL Files in the /docs/fusion folder.

This page also shows the laser cut 1/4" plywood base for the box.

A number of the parts print in specific orientations. Please the 3MF files in the /docs/prusa folder for the exact settings, orientations, and supports that I used.

Overall the printing took just over a full kilogram of grey PETG. I printed the 25 buttons out of clear PLA.
